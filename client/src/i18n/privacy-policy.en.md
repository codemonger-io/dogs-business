# Privacy Policy

⚠️ **THIS IS A DRAFT.** ⚠️

## We identify you with a unique ID

*We* do not know and care who you actually are, but assign a unique ID, *user ID*, to identify you when you sign up to *this app*.
Your *activities* in *this app* are associated with your *user ID*.
*We* do not share *user IDs* with any third parties.

## We collect your locations

*We* collect your *precise location* when *you* submit a *business record*, because *this app* needs it to show *business records* on the *map*.

*We* record your *approximate locations* in our *server logs* while *you* are viewing the *map* in *this app*.

## Regular information for browsing is sent to OpenFreeMap

*This app* uses [*OpenFreeMap*](https://openfreemap.org) as the map provider.
*We* do not share your *user ID* with *OpenFreeMap*, though, your browser will send other regular information including your IP address to *OpenFreeMap* while you are viewing the *map* in *this app*.
Please consult the [privacy policy](https://openfreemap.org/privacy/) of *OpenFreeMap* for more details.

## Data are sent to Amazon Web Services

*We* use services provided by *Amazon Web Services* (*AWS*) to realize and deliver *this app*.
*AWS* may collect certain information as described in their [privacy notice](https://aws.amazon.com/privacy/).
